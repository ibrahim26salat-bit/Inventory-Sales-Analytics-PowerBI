# 📊 Inventory & Sales Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

**Inventory & Sales Analytics** is an interactive **Business Intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, inventory health, warehouse operations, and customer behavior.

The project brings together sales, inventory, product, warehouse, and customer data into a centralized analytical dashboard designed to support business performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard is organized into four analytical pages:

1. **Executive Dashboard**
2. **Sales Analysis**
3. **Inventory Analysis**
4. **Customer Insights**

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to provide management with a centralized view of important business metrics and help identify trends, performance drivers, inventory issues, and customer opportunities.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

* What is the overall revenue and profit performance?
* How are revenue and profit changing over time?
* How many orders have been generated?
* What is the current inventory position?
* Which products have low stock?
* Which products require attention based on reorder levels?
* How is inventory distributed across warehouses?
* Which customers generate the most revenue?
* Which cities contribute to customer activity?
* How does customer revenue change over time?
* Which product categories contribute most to revenue?

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 1. Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard provides a high-level overview of business performance.

### Key KPIs

The page includes KPI cards for:

* **Total Profit**
* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Orders**
* **Inventory Value**

### Analysis

The page provides visual analysis of:

* Revenue and profit trends over time
* Inventory stock status
* Revenue and profit by product category
* Revenue by product
* Revenue by warehouse

This page is designed to give management a quick overview of the overall business situation.

---

# 📈 2. Sales Analysis

The Sales Analysis page focuses on revenue, orders, profitability, customers, and geographical sales performance.

### Key KPIs

* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Orders**
* **Average Order Value**
* **Total Profit**

### Analysis Includes

* Revenue and profit trends over time
* Revenue distribution by city
* Monthly order trends
* Customer revenue ranking

This page helps evaluate sales performance and identify important customer and geographical patterns.

---

# 📦 3. Inventory Analysis

The Inventory Analysis page focuses on inventory availability, stock levels, reorder requirements, and warehouse distribution.

### Key KPIs

* **Products Available**
* **Total Stock Units**
* **Low Stock Products**
* **Active Warehouses**

### Analysis Includes

* Stock status by warehouse
* Current stock vs. reorder level
* Product-level stock status
* Inventory distribution by warehouse

This page helps identify inventory conditions and potential stock management issues.

It can support decisions related to:

* Inventory monitoring
* Replenishment planning
* Low-stock identification
* Warehouse management
* Stock distribution

---

# 🧑‍💼 4. Customer Insights

The Customer Insights page focuses on customer performance, revenue contribution, geographical reach, and customer trends.

### Key KPIs

* **Total Customers**
* **Average Revenue per Customer**
* **Top Customer Revenue**
* **Cities Served**

### Analysis Includes

* Customer revenue ranking
* Customer order/revenue/profit trends
* Customer distribution by city
* Revenue by product category

This page helps identify high-value customers and understand customer-level and geographical performance.
